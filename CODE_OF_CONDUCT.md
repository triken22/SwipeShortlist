# Code Of Conduct

SwipeShortlist contributors are expected to keep discussion practical, respectful, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be specific and constructive in reviews.
- Critique code, product decisions, and evidence without personal attacks.
- Respect privacy and do not post secrets, private links, or personal data.
- Keep issues and pull requests scoped to the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or discriminatory remarks.
- Publishing private credentials, deployment details, or user data.
- Repeated off-topic comments after maintainers ask to refocus.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.
